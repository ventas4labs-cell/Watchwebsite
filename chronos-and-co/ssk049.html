Presage Cocktail Time GMT

Presage Cocktail Time GMT U.S. Special Creation. Offering the finest in Japanese mechanical watchmaking, the Presage Collection blends precision technology with superior craftsmanship and design. Evoking the Silver Bullet cocktail, the vintage-tone silver dial features a deep pressed pattern with a lustrous gloss finish, accented with a deep blue GMT hand designed in the shape of a cocktail glass and a gold-tone second hand. Beautifully displaying a blue cursive-style Arabic numeral 24-hour scale, this sophisticated creation is powered by a 24-jewel automatic GMT movement, visible through the screwdown see-through caseback, beating at a frequency of 21,600 vibrations per hour with a power reserve of approximately 41 hours. Crafted of stainless steel, this distinctive timepiece includes a date calendar, box-shaped crystal, and a dark blue leather strap with a tri-fold push button release clasp. 5 bar, 50M Water Resistant
